Composio now charges Pro organizations for regular tool usage. For now, Composio does not block your organization when its balance goes negative. We recommend turning on auto reload and recharges so your balance stays positive.

### What's changing

- **Pro organizations are charged for regular tool usage.** Charging started on 2026-11-24.
- **No blocking on a negative balance, for now.** Your organization keeps running tool calls even if its balance is negative.
- **Turn on auto reload.** We recommend that organizations opt in to auto reload and recharges.

### Hobby plan

Hobby organizations that have used more than 100k tool calls and have not upgraded to Pro are rate limited more aggressively, starting 2026-11-24.
