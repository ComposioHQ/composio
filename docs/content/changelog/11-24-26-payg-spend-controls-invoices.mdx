Pay-as-you-go billing now has controls for watching and limiting what your organization spends. You can set spend alerts, see your daily cost by product, read current and past usage invoices, and manage the cards available for auto reload.

### What's new

- **Spend controls.** Get notified when billing-cycle spend reaches a threshold. Add a spend alert with a dollar amount and an email recipient. Turn on **Stops paid calls** for an alert that should also stop paid calls. You can edit or delete an alert at any time.
- **Cost by product.** A daily cost chart for a billing period, with totals for gross usage, credits applied, and overage, broken down by product such as Instant Usage. Use the period picker to switch between periods, including the current draft invoice period.
- **Usage invoices.** Current and past usage statements for your organization. Each statement lists line items as quantity times unit price: Instant Usage and Platform standard tool calls (Tier 1 and Tier 2), followed by credits applied and usage beyond credits.
- **Payment methods.** See the cards available for auto reload and add a new payment method.

### How usage invoices work

The usage statement for the current period is a draft, and its numbers change until the period is finalized. Past periods appear as finalized. Wallet top-ups and plan payments appear in payment history, and the usage statement is separate from payment receipts.
