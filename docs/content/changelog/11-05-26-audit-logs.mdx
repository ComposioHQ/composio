Organization admins can now see who changed their organization and when in **Organization Settings > Audit logs**. Each event names the member who made the change, describes it in one sentence, and shows the IP address and source of the request, such as the dashboard, an API key, or the CLI.

### What's new

- Audit logs record changes to the organization, its members and invites, projects and project settings, API keys, webhook subscriptions, and session configs.
- Changes that Composio makes, such as a plan change by Composio support, appear with **Composio** as the member.
- You can filter events by time range, member, and event type.
- On the Hobby plan, audit logs cover the last 30 days. On paid plans, they cover the last 12 months.

See [Audit logs](/docs/security/audit-logs) for what audit logs record and what they don't.
