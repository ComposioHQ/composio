The **Don't store data** option under **Log storage** is now **Zero Data Retention** (ZDR). You manage it as an organization add-on in **Organization Settings > Billing > Add-ons**. Every project kept its setting, so projects that had **Don't store data** on now show ZDR on.

### What's new

- An organization admin can turn on ZDR for all projects or for selected projects. Admins can also turn it on or off for one project in **Project Settings > General**.
- Paid plans can add ZDR for a usage-based price, and the Enterprise plan includes it. It is not available on the free plan. Projects on the free plan that already had **Don't store data** on keep ZDR on. An admin can turn it off, and turning it back on requires a paid plan.
- The [Zero Data Retention](/docs/security/zero-data-retention) page lists what ZDR covers, what it does not cover, and what Composio still stores.
