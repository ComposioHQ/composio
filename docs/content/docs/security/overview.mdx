## Compliance and the Trust Center

Start with the [Composio Trust Center](https://trust.composio.dev) for current compliance reports and the [subprocessor register](https://trust.composio.dev/subprocessors). Review the [Data Processing Addendum](https://composio.dev/legal/dpa) and [Business Associate Agreement](https://composio.dev/legal/baa) for the terms that apply to your workload.

Read the [vendor-review guidance](/kb/guide/platform-compliance-data-handling) for data-processing terms, model-training requirements, and enterprise reviews.

## Credential protection

Provider credentials are encrypted at rest using AES-256-GCM, and traffic uses TLS. Review [token custody architecture](/docs/security/token-custody) for how Composio stores and uses credentials, what API redaction covers, and which [deployment options](/docs/security/token-custody#deployment-options) meet your custody requirements.

## Isolation and access control

Check [API-key permissions](/reference/authenticating-to-composio/project-api-key-permissions) and [provider scopes](/docs/authentication/controlling-scopes) against the operations your application needs. Use [audit logs](/docs/security/audit-logs) to review administrative changes and [outbound IP addresses](/docs/security/outbound-ips) to configure network allowlists.

## Your responsibilities

You authenticate your users and decide which accounts and tools they can access. Review those choices alongside your application's own logs and model-provider policies.

## Related resources

- Read [data retention and handling](/docs/security/data-retention) to map logs, files, model processing, and downstream providers.
- Review [Zero Data Retention](/docs/security/zero-data-retention) before enabling it. Check coverage and exclusions against your workload. ZDR controls storage of covered payloads, not processing during execution.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Report vulnerabilities privately to [security@composio.dev](mailto:security@composio.dev).
