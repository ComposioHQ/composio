### Credential protection

Provider credentials are encrypted at rest using AES-256-GCM, and traffic uses TLS. Composio uses credentials during execution. ZDR controls storage of covered payloads, not processing during execution.

### Your responsibilities

You authenticate your users and decide which accounts and tools they can access. Review those choices alongside your application's own logs and model-provider policies.
