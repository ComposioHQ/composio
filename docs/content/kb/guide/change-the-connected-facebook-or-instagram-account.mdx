A Facebook, Page, or Instagram connection authenticates whichever account was selected in Meta's own account picker during authorization. That choice is recorded by Meta, so it cannot be changed from the Composio side afterwards.

To connect a different account, the authorization has to be redone from Meta:

1. Remove the existing Composio/Meta app authorization from Facebook's Business Integrations settings.
2. Start the connection flow again.
3. Select the intended account, Page, or Instagram profile in Meta's picker.

Deleting and recreating the Composio connected account without clearing the authorization on Meta's side will usually reconnect the same account, because Meta reuses the prior grant.
